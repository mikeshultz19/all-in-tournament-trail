## AITT Admin Center

The staff application is separate from public navigation:

```text
Admin Center
  ├── Home
  ├── Tournament Manager
  │     ├── Prepare Tournament
  │     ├── Import and Reconcile WeighFish Results
  │     ├── Insurance Pot
  │     ├── Calculate Payouts / Closeout
  │     ├── Publish Results
  │     └── Calculate AOY
  ├── Tournament Information
  ├── Members
  ├── Registration Review
  ├── Results
  ├── Announcements
  ├── Rules
  ├── FAQ
  ├── Settings
  └── Website Analytics
        └── Registration Interest
```

The dashboard is scoped to a selected Current Tournament. Sponsors are not a
tournament readiness item or management card.

## Global

- Shared header
- Shared footer
- Shared `PageHeader` component
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Social links

## Explicit Exclusions

- No separate archive image gallery
- No separate Big Bass results page
- No separate safety requirements page
- No extra routes without approval
