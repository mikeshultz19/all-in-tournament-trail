# AITT Competition Rules

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Approved:** July 29, 2026  

This document is the authoritative constitutional specification for AITT
Competition Structure, Competitive Records, Angler of the Year, and
Championship qualification. The separate Official Tournament Rules govern
event operations, safety, fishing conduct, penalties, and related procedures.

## 1. Competition Structure

- AITT operates one tournament trail.
- AITT has one Angler of the Year competition.
- AITT has one Championship.
- Solo and Team are tournament registration types, not separate season-long
  divisions.
- Every tournament registration establishes the Competitive Record that
  receives all season credit from that registration.
- Two Competitive Record types exist:
  - Team Competitive Record
  - Solo Competitive Record
- The registration type selected for a tournament determines which Competitive
  Record receives AOY points, Championship participation, tournament history,
  wins, statistics, and awards.
- Team and Solo Competitive Records are completely independent.
- Results never transfer between Competitive Records.

## 2. Team Identity

### Rules

1. A Team consists of two specifically identified anglers.
2. Team identity belongs to stable angler records rather than a typed
   display-name string.
3. Reversing partner order does not create a different Team.

   Example:

   - `John Smith / Mike Jones`
   - `Mike Jones / John Smith`

   These represent the same Team.

4. AOY points, Championship participation credit, and season history assigned
   to a Team belong to that stable pairing.
5. A pairing of one established Team member with a different partner is a new
   Team.
6. When both anglers are eligible, a new pairing may begin its own Team record
   immediately.
7. A new Team inherits no AOY points, Championship participation credit, or
   tournament history from either angler's other Team.
8. A Team tournament registration requires Angler 2.
9. Current registration instructions require the established season partner to
   be entered even when that partner is unable to fish the tournament.
10. A Team registration creates or uses the identified Team Competitive
    Record.
11. Both identified anglers are members of that Competitive Record regardless
    of whether one partner is physically present during the tournament.
12. All AOY points, Championship qualification, participation history, wins,
    and statistics earned from the registration belong to that Team
    Competitive Record.

## 3. Solo Participation

### Rules

1. A competitor may fish a tournament alone.
2. A Solo registration contains one angler.
3. Angler 2 is prohibited from a Solo registration and must not be submitted.
4. A Solo competitor fishes in the same official tournament field under the
   same tournament, safety, fishing, scoring, and results rules.
5. A Solo competitor does not create a separate Solo AOY division or Solo
   Championship system.
6. Current registration instructions state that a Solo registration applies
   only to that tournament and does not create a separate season-long division.
7. The Official Tournament Rules state that a Solo member may receive
   member-only benefits when all applicable requirements are satisfied.
8. A Solo registration creates or uses the angler's Solo Competitive Record.
9. A Solo Competitive Record is completely independent of every Team
   Competitive Record.
10. All AOY points, Championship qualification, participation history, wins,
    and statistics earned from a Solo registration belong exclusively to the
    Solo Competitive Record.
11. Solo results never transfer to a Team Competitive Record.
12. Team results never transfer to a Solo Competitive Record.
13. The competitor selects the Competitive Record at registration.
14. That Competitive Record selection is final once registration closes.

## 4. AOY Points

### Rules

1. There is one AOY competition.
2. AOY is calculated separately from Official Results.
3. AOY standings are calculated independently from Championship
   qualification.
4. AOY uses published Official Results as its source.
5. Membership and AOY eligibility must never alter official finishing order,
   weights, Big Bass, payouts, or public Official Results.
6. Only eligible Competitive Records may earn AOY points.
7. Both anglers on a two-person Team must satisfy the applicable membership
   requirements for the Team to be AOY eligible, including when one identified
   partner is unable to fish.
8. First Eligible Tournament is the business control for membership
   eligibility. Membership Effective Date is administrative only.
9. After eligibility is determined:
   1. Begin with the complete official finishing order.
   2. Remove ineligible entries from the AOY calculation only.
   3. Preserve Official Results unchanged.
   4. Rerank the eligible entries in their original relative finishing order.
   5. Award points from the reranked AOY position.
10. Ineligible competitors include, but are not limited to:
   - Non-members
   - Competitors ineligible for AOY
   - Competitors ineligible for Championship qualification
   - Competitors ineligible for Bronze, Silver, Gold, Insurance, or other
     member-only season awards
11. The documented AOY schedule is:
   - 1st eligible AOY position: 200 points
   - 2nd eligible AOY position: 199 points
   - 3rd eligible AOY position: 198 points
   - Each following eligible position receives one fewer point.
12. An eligible entry that participates and records zero tournament weight
    receives 10 participation points.
13. A no-show receives 0 AOY points.
14. A disqualified entry receives 0 AOY points.
15. Participation must actually occur for a zero-weight entry to receive 10
    points.
16. Wins and Top 10s are based on reranked AOY finish, not official tournament
    finish.
17. A zero-weight score counts as a Top 10 only if the entry's actual reranked
    AOY finish is within the Top 10.
18. AOY ties are documented as being resolved in this order:
    1. Most AOY tournament wins
    2. Most AOY Top 10 finishes
    3. Highest total official season weight from all AOY-eligible appearances
    4. Compare tied records' AOY finishes from the most recent regular-season
       tournament backward until the tie is broken
19. The regular season consists of eight scheduled tournaments.
20. Each Competitive Record's final AOY score is the sum of its five highest
    point totals from those eight tournaments.
21. The three lowest point totals, including tournaments not fished, are
    automatically discarded.
22. The eligible Competitive Record with the highest best-five total is the
    Angler of the Year, subject to the documented tie breakers.
23. The Championship does not award AOY points.
24. AOY is finalized after the final regular-season tournament.
25. AOY credit is assigned to the Team or Solo Competitive Record selected by
    the tournament registration.
26. AOY credit never transfers between Team and Solo Competitive Records.

### AOY reranking example

Official Finish:

1. Non-member
2. Team A
3. Team B

The Official Results remain unchanged. For AOY:

1. Team A receives 1st-place AOY points.
2. Team B receives 2nd-place AOY points.

This reranking affects only season standings and award calculations. It never
changes the published Official Tournament Results.

## 5. Championship Qualification

### Rules

1. There is one Championship qualification system.
2. Championship qualification is separate from AOY standings and AOY rank.
3. The regular season consists of eight scheduled tournaments.
4. A Competitive Record qualifies by competing in at least five of those eight
   regular-season tournaments.
5. Championship qualification is based solely on participation.
6. AOY points do not determine Championship eligibility.
7. A qualifying participation requires actual tournament participation.
8. Payment or registration without participation does not count.
9. A no-show does not count.
10. A disqualification does not count.
11. An appearance using a different partner does not count for the original
   Team.
12. An entry that fails the applicable eligibility requirements does not count.
13. First Eligible Tournament controls membership eligibility; Membership
    Effective Date is administrative only.
14. A new Team inherits no Championship participation credit from either
    angler's other Team.
15. Championship qualification is locked after the final qualifying
    tournament.
16. A record may:
    - Win or lead AOY without qualifying for the Championship.
    - Qualify for the Championship without leading or winning AOY.
17. The Championship is the final event of the season.
18. The Championship is a two-day tournament.
19. The Championship does not award AOY points.
20. Membership remains active through the Championship.
21. The season ends after the Championship is completed.
22. Championship participation is assigned to the Team or Solo Competitive
    Record selected by the tournament registration.
23. Championship participation never transfers between Team and Solo
    Competitive Records.

## 6. Partner Changes

### Rules

1. Substitute partners are not recognized as continuations of an established
   Team.
2. If an angler fishes with someone other than the established Team partner,
   the pairing is a new Team.
3. The new pairing:
   - Appears normally in Official Results.
   - Does not earn AOY points for the original Team.
   - Does not count toward the original Team's Championship qualification.
   - Does not provide Member Side Pot eligibility to the original Team.
   - May establish its own Team record when otherwise eligible.
4. The new Team inherits no AOY points, Championship participation credit, or
   season history from either angler's other Team.
5. Partner order does not create a new Team.
6. The Tournament Director interprets and enforces the published Competition
   Rules.
7. The Tournament Director may correct administrative, clerical, identity,
   software, and mathematical errors.
8. The Tournament Director may not waive, alter, or create a competition rule
   unless these Rules expressly authorize Director discretion.
9. A correction must preserve the integrity and fairness of the competition
   and must be documented.

## 7. Dropped Tournaments

### Rules

1. Every eligible tournament AOY score is retained in tournament-level AOY
   history.
2. Only the five highest AOY tournament scores contribute to the season AOY
   total.
3. The regular season consists of eight scheduled tournaments.
4. Each Competitive Record has eight possible regular-season point totals.
5. A tournament not fished is included as a lowest point total for purposes of
   the best-five calculation.
6. The three lowest point totals are automatically discarded.
7. Scores outside the best five remain visible but do not contribute to the
   season AOY total.
8. There is no minimum tournament requirement to win AOY.
9. Championship participation is separate from the best-five AOY calculation.
10. Total Season Weight for the AOY tie breaker includes all AOY-eligible
   tournament appearances, not only the five scores included in the AOY total.
11. Wins and Top 10s from every AOY-eligible tournament remain part of the
    Competitive Record's season statistics and tie-break totals, including
    tournaments whose point scores are outside the best five.

## 8. Official Results

### Rules

1. The complete official WeighFish CSV becomes the official tournament field
   after import and review.
2. Walk-in entries recorded in WeighFish remain part of the official field.
3. Official Results preserve the complete finishing order, weights, entry
   names, Big Bass results, and payouts.
4. Membership and AOY eligibility do not reorder, filter, or modify Official
   Results.
5. Tournament Results become Official only after:
   - Weigh-in is complete.
   - All penalties have been assessed.
   - All protests have been resolved.
   - The Tournament Director authorizes publication.
6. AOY points are generated separately after required membership and identity
   reconciliation.
7. Official Results and AOY results remain separate sources of information.
8. Any AOY recalculation must be deterministic and must not silently alter
   Official Results.
9. Once published as Official Results, finishing positions are final and
   immutable.
10. Official Results are never modified to reflect membership eligibility or
    AOY calculations.
11. AOY standings are calculated independently from published Official
    Results.
12. After publication, only a documented administrative, clerical,
    mathematical, identity, or software error may be corrected.
13. Every permitted post-publication correction must be documented by the
    Tournament Director.

## 9. Exceptions

### Rules

1. A no-show receives 0 AOY points and no Championship participation credit.
2. A disqualified entry receives 0 AOY points and no Championship
   participation credit.
3. An eligible entry must actually participate to receive the 10-point
   zero-weight participation award.
4. Official Results remain separate from any AOY points awarded.
5. The Tournament Director may delay, shorten, postpone, cancel, or reschedule
   an event because of unsafe weather, water, ramp, facility, or government
   conditions.
6. Tournament Officials have final authority over safety decisions, penalties,
   eligibility, protests, and disqualifications under the Official Tournament
   Rules.
7. Administrative exceptions must be explicitly approved, documented, dated,
   attributed to the approving person, and auditable.
8. The software must not automatically assume an exception.
9. A Director exception may not override membership requirements except to
   correct a documented administrative error, according to the AOY
   specification.
10. The Tournament Director may correct administrative, clerical, identity,
    software, and mathematical errors.
11. The Tournament Director may not waive, alter, or create a competition rule
    unless these Rules expressly authorize Director discretion.
12. Every correction must preserve competition integrity and fairness and must
    be documented.
13. A postponed tournament remains the same scheduled tournament.
14. The Tournament Director may reschedule it to a later date before the
    Championship, including at the end of the regular-season schedule.
15. Rescheduling changes only the calendar date. It does not change the
    tournament's identity or original tournament number.
16. The original tournament number continues to govern tournament records, AOY
    calculations, Championship participation, First Eligible Tournament
    ordering, and historical records.

Example: If Tournament #3 is postponed and conducted after Tournament #8, it
remains Tournament #3 for all competition purposes.

### Withdrawal

1. A competitor who begins tournament competition is considered to have
   competed.
2. Withdrawal after competition begins does not negate participation.
3. The tournament counts toward the selected Competitive Record's Championship
   participation.
4. AOY treatment follows the published Official Result, including the
   zero-weight participation rule when applicable.
5. No refund is issued after competition begins.

### Cancelled Tournament

If competition never begins:

- No Official Results are produced.
- No AOY points are awarded.
- No Championship participation is earned.

### Shortened Tournament

1. The Tournament Director may shorten tournament hours.
2. Official weigh-in occurs at the revised time.
3. Published Official Results determine final standings.
4. The event counts as a completed regular-season tournament.
